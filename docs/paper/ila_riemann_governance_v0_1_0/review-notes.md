# Review and Revision Notes

- The original draft was rewritten as a technical note rather than a proof attempt.
- Strong proof-like claims were replaced with heuristic and conceptual claims.
- The Euler product is explicitly treated as an identity only for Re(s) > 1; inside the critical strip the paper uses finite prime sums and regularization procedures.
- The random-walk scaling was refined into a weighted prime-mode energy model E_N(sigma)=sum p^{-2 sigma}.
- The critical line is presented as a phase boundary under the random-phase heuristic, not as a proven zero-location theorem.
- Reviewer feedback was incorporated by adding heuristic boundary notes to major sections.
- Circularity risks around RH-equivalent error terms were expanded into an explicit warning and conditional/non-circular alternatives.
- The explicit-formula bridge was made more concrete by adding a standard psi(x) explicit formula and a checklist for compatibility conditions.
- A next-version implementation reference hook was added for GitHub links, commit hashes, and function-to-interface mapping tables.
- Appendix A lists potentially misleading expressions and safer replacements.
- References support the Riemann Hypothesis, zeta function theory, explicit formula context, spectral analogies, ergodic/random-phase motivation, and ILA terminology.

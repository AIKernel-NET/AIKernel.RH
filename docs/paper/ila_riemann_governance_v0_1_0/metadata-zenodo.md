## Description

This technical note presents a conceptual and heuristic system-governance interpretation of the Riemann Hypothesis through Interface-Led Architecture. It does not claim to prove the Riemann Hypothesis. The paper maps primes to oscillatory Providers, zeta zeros to cancellation contracts, and the critical line to a governance boundary between divergent and over-damped prime-mode phases.

This reviewed edition strengthens the non-proof boundary by adding heuristic notes to major sections, explicit circularity warnings around RH-equivalent error estimates, a more concrete bridge to Riemann's explicit formula for the Chebyshev function, and a short appendix of potentially misleading expressions and safer replacements. It also notes that implementation references, GitHub links, commit hashes, and function-to-interface mapping tables are deferred to a future version.

The English manuscript is the canonical version. The Japanese manuscript is included as a companion translation.
